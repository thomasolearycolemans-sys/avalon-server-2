The gist of Avalon

Good and Evil sit at one table. Good outnumbers Evil, but doesn't know who's who — Evil knows each other. Good wins by succeeding three quests; Evil wins by failing three, or by staying hidden.

1 · Propose The leader picks a small team to go on the quest.
2 · Vote Everyone approves or rejects that team — votes are public. Reject five in a row and Evil wins, so teams must eventually pass.
3 · Quest Only the chosen team plays a card in secret. Good must play Success. Evil may play Success or Fail. One Fail usually sinks the quest (some large quests need two).
4 · Repeat Leadership passes around the table. First side to three quests wins the quests.
5 · The dagger If Good succeeds three quests, Evil's Assassin gets one last chance: name Merlin. Guess right, and Evil steals the win.

Your job is to read the table — who proposed whom, who voted how, who was aboard a failed quest. The History panel remembers all of it for you.
